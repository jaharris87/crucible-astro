## Discoverable

Purpose: provide a finite benchmark for reproducing the pinned native XNet
instantaneous RHS at unchanged full-composition states. **24,314 states from
380 histories**, one maintainer-identified Chimera D9.6-SN160-3D parent. This is
neither a rectangular physical validity domain nor representative CCSN/NSM data.

The [raw collection](genesis_datacard_ccsn_raw_candidate.md), original pilot,
[quarantined union](genesis_datacard_ccsn_primary_native_rhs_union_v0_1.md) and this
qualified view have separate identities. Original-state historical generating
build association remains incomplete. Fresh labels have separate actual pinned
source/build/run chains. Maintainer attribution credits Eric Lentz for CHIMERA
simulation data; Raph Hix and J. Austin Harris for XNet; Andre Sieverding for
postprocessing tooling; James Ternullo for this workflow execution. See
[parent/provenance evidence](../phase1/PARENT_PROVENANCE.md).

## Interoperable

The [payload contract](../phase1/PRIMARY_RHS_DATASET.md) preserves full ordered
Y and fresh F, time (s), T9 (GK), rho (g/cm³), native EOS Ye and source identities.
Y is the native molar abundance convention; F has Y/s units. Mass-fraction rates
are A*F. Native Ye is sum(ZY)/sum(AY); no normalization, clipping or fixed Ye.
Weak/screening are retained, heating is off and active neutrino rates are zero.
Stored derivatives and internal-step energy remain audit-only, not accepted
training targets. No interval/rollout target is inferred from saved timesteps.

The new overlay joins exact canonical record IDs to the immutable payload and
accepted memberships, binding actual human receipt, source references, scoring,
exclusions and qualification evidence. It grants state/RHS uses solely for this
named benchmark; energy/rollout eligibility stays false. The payload's four stored
masks remain false: consumers must resolve the overlay explicitly, never rewrite
or reinterpret the historical masks. [The qualification record](../phase1/PHASE1_CLOSEOUT.md)
describes identities, recipes and checks without duplicating private inventories.

## Governed Use

Qualification covers the declared numerical target and finite membership only.
It establishes neither historical-build equivalence, nuclear/EOS/astrophysical
accuracy nor integrated abundance/yield/heating accuracy. D-022 physical-change
floors/scales/timescales remain unresolved for those later purposes. Dataset access
and release authority remain separate from scientific suitability.
