# Expanded within-parent benchmark: scope and grouping proposal

2026-11-17, [S03 #15](https://github.com/jaharris87/crucible-astro/issues/15).
**Proposed covariate allocation, not accepted partitions, qualified labels or
final-test authority.** This is one fixed proposal following the maintainer's
decision to expand the primary audit and retain the 80-history qualification pilot.

## Intended claim and membership

Propose pointwise fidelity to the D-019 pinned native XNet RHS for the exact
[380-history candidate pool](PRIMARY_EXPANSION_AUDIT.md). Each eligible saved
state is an example with unchanged full-160 Y and recorded T9/rho. Conditions,
species/rates/EOS and exclusions remain explicit; native Ye comes from Y.
Stored derivatives remain audit-only. The task is instantaneous; energy,
finite-step and rollout targets are excluded. Positions inform grouping, not
the RHS inputs. No method, fitted transform or model result enters this proposal.

This asks about held-out operational proximity groups **within one parent
simulation**. It does not ask about a new simulation, independent physical
families, universal in-domain approximation or production CCSN/NSM accuracy.
Representative sampling and sufficient joint coverage are not established.
The finite population and rule must be version-bound; the pilot grouping is not
inherited. No related raw/derived copy or history may cross proposed roles.

## Rule fixed before role-dependent coverage

1. Use the 94 measured-nearest connected components from the frozen expanded
   pool, as disclosed operational correlation controls rather than physical
   families. The [audit](PRIMARY_EXPANSION_AUDIT.md) records the metric, measured
   time window, changing pilot links and residual proximity.
2. Anchor components containing global T9/rho/native-Ye minima/maxima in training,
   with canonical particle/ordinal ties. In fixed row-then-category order, add the
   minimum fixed-hash component for any stratum still absent from training.
3. Within each existing homogeneous category, set validation and test quotas
   each to `max(1, floor(0.15 * category_component_count))`. Rank nonanchor
   components by one fixed SHA256 over namespace, sorted canonical members and
   literal protocol tag. First quota goes to validation, next to test, remainder
   to training. Anchors stay training; insufficient support fails without fallback.

This deliberate input-based curation keeps pooled scalar extrema and each known
row/category represented in training. It is necessary support, not a proof of
full-composition coverage. The exact namespace/encoding/tag, inputs, ties and
membership remain in the bound local protocol; no seed search or allocation
retuning occurred. This data-specific rule is not prescribed by ML literature.
Later preprocessing fitting must use training membership only.

## Full-state and condition support

Every one of the 6,655 proposed held-out states was compared with every one of
the 17,659 proposed training states. Use full-160 unnormalized X=A*Y L1 distance
and, separately, Euclidean distance in unweighted log10(T9)/log10(rho). No fitted
scaling or physical threshold is introduced. Conditions/composition at every
nearest match and exact identities remain in the private all-point table.

Training encloses scalar T9/rho/Ye ranges by curation. Nevertheless, 156 validation
states exceed training component envelopes across 27 species; 135 test states
do so across 12 species. No positive held-out species is entirely absent from
training. These strict comparisons impose no abundance floor or sufficiency
threshold. Nearest composition and nearest conditions are often different
training points; neither separate match establishes joint physical support.

Compressed histories remain extremely close in composition across roles:
nearest-training X medians approximately 1.61e-5 / 2.30e-5 and minima 7.64e-15 /
1.43e-14 for validation/test. Distinct bound particles are not exact known raw
copies, but correlations remain and can make that portion easy. Report categories
and trajectory/group denominators separately; do not treat all timesteps as
independent evidence or claim independent-family generalization.

All proposed nearest spatial links stay whole. Cross-role minimum median spatial
metric is 0.096596 and minimum sampled-frame approach 0.004275, with no accepted
physical separation cutoff. 8,714 candidate states follow the measured position
window; whole-history IDs remain assigned without inventing late coordinates.

## Qualification and acceptance boundary

The proposal supplies a concrete grouping/membership choice for human scientific
consideration. It does not itself accept sufficient coverage, qualify references,
select a surrogate error budget or permit final-test use. The
[benchmark decision sheet](BENCHMARK.md) retains the task/domain/observable,
metric aggregation, justified tolerance and reference-acceptance gates. Scientific
adequacy and software merge are separate decisions. Phase 2 remains unauthorized.

Before final use, implement and verify the accepted partition rule, including
deliberate related-copy/duplicate rejection and empty/unknown-qualified-membership
failures. Fitted preprocessing belongs to training only when it exists; no Phase 2
stack or dummy fitter is introduced in Phase 1.

Initial private implementation failures were fingerprint byte-order assertion
and NumPy metadata serialization errors. Original sources/logs/outputs are
preserved; narrowly named repairs change no allocation rule, and every repeated
nearest-match result is byte-identical. Same-worker independent-specification
allocation recomputation and seven exhaustive scalar query checks pass; this
is not substituted for fresh PR review.
