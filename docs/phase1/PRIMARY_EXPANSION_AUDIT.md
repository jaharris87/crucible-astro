## What proximity establishes

For comparison with the pilot only, apply the same disclosed diagnostic metric:
the median over measured frames of Cartesian separation divided by sum of radii.
Link each history to its nearest other history, with canonical-ID ties, and take
undirected connected components. The expanded pool has **94 diagnostic
components**, sizes 2–13. Ninety contain Ejected histories; four contain the
16 Compressed histories (4/6/3/3 histories and 169/245/131/126 candidate states).
These are operational proximity components, not demonstrated independent families.

Expansion changes 47 of the old 80 nearest links and splits 14 of the old 21
pilot components across new components. The old groups and allocation therefore
cannot be inherited as primary physical families. Any accepted finite grouping
must bind its population/version and intended within-parent claim explicitly.

Residual proximity crosses components: minimum history-median metric 0.09660;
closest sampled-frame normalized approach 0.003156. Only 134 of the 1,803 pooled
nearest-X matches share a diagnostic component. Similar pointwise inputs can
occur at different measured positions. Similarity does not itself establish
common raw ancestry; component separation does not prove physical independence.
No physical distance cutoff or primary group assignment is accepted here.
The [single expanded allocation proposal](PRIMARY_BENCHMARK_PROPOSAL.md) binds
one proposed use of these operational groups and reports all-point coverage
against its proposed training membership. It is not an accepted physical-family
claim or a production scientific split check.
