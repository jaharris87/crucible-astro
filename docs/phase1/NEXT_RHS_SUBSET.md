## Accepted numerical-reference contract (D-019)

Use the selected pinned 160-species XNet kernel as a **new declared numerical
reference** at each unchanged emitted Y. Preserve ordered `(Z,A)` and `X=A*Y`.
Conditions are recorded T9 (GK) and density (g/cm³); time remains source identity.
Ye is composition-derived, not fixed or replaced with history Ye. Retain weak/
screening physics, heating off and the asserted zero active neutrino-rate count.
Bind source, rates, EOS/includes/tables, precision and build. Stop rather than
silently substituting dependencies or disabling physics.

The target is an instantaneous vector RHS. Energy, finite-step and rollout
qualification are excluded from this pilot. Keep their fields and limitations
separate. D-004/D-005 pilot contract choices do not establish actual named-subset
qualification, constraints/tolerances, historical equivalence or astrophysical
accuracy. Final D-007 reference adequacy and benchmark acceptance remain open.

## Original execution proposal: deferred

The original proposal was to prepare/review an evaluator and then evaluate
exactly the 147 listed states, including the two in-grid pilot states as controls
under one dataset build. **The maintainer deferred that execution.** First finish
the read-only multi-trajectory candidate audit, revise the exact evaluation scope
and request separate authorization. No new evaluator build/run is authorized.
D-017 is exhausted by its three completed evaluations.

Any later authorized harness must preserve each input bit, species identity and
configuration. Retain all fresh components, state echoes, raw lineage, exclusions,
numerical diagnostics, compiler/library/executable/runtime evidence and failures
in a new local artifact and linked card. Original raw and quarantined HDF/manifest
bytes remain unchanged. No mask becomes true automatically. Stop on mismatch,
missing dependency, changed physics, nonfinite values, build/run failure or
completion. Exact-candidate independent review and human merge remain required.

## Multi-trajectory benchmark gates

D-018 requires a **multi-trajectory training pool** and separate defensible
trajectory/family groups for validation and testing. Each qualified saved state
is an individual pointwise example; one history can contribute many correlated
examples. Assess the relevant joint full-composition/T/rho/regime coverage of the
pooled training candidates. One history, Ye overlap or scalar extrema cannot
establish sufficient coverage.

C was initially considered as a separate composition-shift challenge relative to
A/B. Its eventual role must follow coverage of the larger pool; do not force its
earlier proposed role. Keep related histories and all raw/derived copies together.
No scientific partition or final-test use exists. Before final acceptance, bind
qualified membership/exclusions, verify accepted group/lineage disjointness,
select observables and aggregation, and justify reference/scientific tolerances.
Exact serialization checks do not establish astrophysical accuracy. Train-only
preprocessing rules apply without building the Phase 2 stack. Limited exploration
is not full training qualification or Phase 1 completion.
