## Scientific contract proposed for acceptance

Use the selected, fingerprinted 160-species XNet kernel as a **new declared
numerical reference** at each unchanged emitted Y. Preserve ordered `(Z,A)` and
the source convention `X=A*Y`; no silent reordering or chemical conversion.
Conditions are recorded T9 (GK) and density (g/cm³), with time carried as source
identity. Ye is composition-derived, not fixed or replaced with history Ye.
Preserve weak/screening controls, heating off, and the asserted zero active
neutrino-rate count. Bind all source, nuclear/rate, EOS/include/table and build
inputs. Stop rather than disabling physics or substituting dependencies.

This proposes an instantaneous vector RHS target, not a finite-step update,
trajectory reconstruction, integrated solution, deposited-heating target or
deployment domain. Recommend excluding energy and rollout qualification from
this pilot target; keep their fields/limitations explicit. Full scientific
D-004/D-005 acceptance and D-007 reference/benchmark/tolerance acceptance remain
separate from permission to implement or execute.

## Benchmark and tolerance gates

One possible named pilot is within-parent cross-trajectory exploration, with A
training, B validation and C held out, **if the maintainer accepts that estimand
and its correlations**. All records and derived copies from a trajectory stay
together. These three purposive samples are not independent simulations or a
representative CCSN population; related-family uncertainty must remain visible.
Alternative: keep all three as development data and recover additional justified
holdout groups before selecting scientific memberships.

Neither alternative is selected here. No scientific partition or final-test use
exists. Before final acceptance, bind exact qualified membership and exclusions,
verify disjoint lineage/groups, select observables/aggregation and justify actual
reference/reproduction and scientific tolerances. Fixed-binary input/serialization
checks can be exact; they do not establish astrophysical accuracy. Train-only
preprocessing rules apply without introducing a Phase 2 training stack. Limited
exploration must not be relabeled full training qualification or Phase 1 completion.
